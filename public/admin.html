Critical verification rule
Customer UPI app redirects and “I've paid” clicks are not proof of receipt. The built-in admin reconciliation requires checking the bank statement and entering a UTR. For automatic status updates, connect a trusted PSP/bank settlement feed to the HMAC-signed settlement endpoint.
Normal/static QR
